Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026 • Effective Date: November 17, 2026

Welcome to TypeRush ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through this Privacy Policy. This document explains how our mobile application handles user information across Android (Google Play Store) and iOS (Apple App Store).

Summary: TypeRush does not require account creation, logins, or cloud synchronization. All typing tests, scores, history, and unlocked palettes stay locally on your device. We use Google AdMob to display ads, which collects standardized mobile advertising identifiers according to your privacy consent choices.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

To provide a safe, frictionless experience, TypeRush never asks for or collects:

  • Personal identifying details (e.g., your name, home address, or phone number).
  • Account credentials (there is no user registration, password, or profile system).
  • Financial or credit card details (all rewarded features are earned via video ads).
  • Biometric data, contacts, camera recordings, or microphone audio.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

TypeRush stores functional performance data directly on your device using high-performance, encrypted MMKV local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us:

  • Typing Speed Metrics: Net Words Per Minute (WPM), Raw WPM, typing accuracy percentage, error counts, and test durations.
  • Historical Records: Completed typing tests, progression logs, and calculated skill tiers.
  • App Preferences: Selected theme (Dark, Light, System), active color palette (Emerald, Cyberpunk, Synthwave, Monokai Pro, Dracula, Nord), font sizes, sound effects, and haptic feedback toggles.
  • Theme Unlocks: Digital unlock status of bonus theme palettes unlocked by watching rewarded videos.

You can permanently erase all local records at any time by navigating to Settings > Clear All History inside the app.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

TypeRush is supported by advertising delivered through Google AdMob (using the react-native-google-mobile-ads framework). While TypeRush itself does not collect personal identifiers, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect certain device data to serve advertisements:

  • Mobile Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID) on Android devices, and Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iOS (subject to user permission).
  • Technical Device Information: Device model, operating system version, carrier, and screen dimensions.
  • Coarse Location Data: IP address used to approximate general geographic area (such as country or city level) for relevant regional ads.
  • Ad Interaction Metrics: Information regarding ad impressions, views, clicks, and diagnostic performance logs.

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services

4. User Consent & Privacy Controls (Google UMP)

We respect your right to control ad personalization:

  • European Regulations (GDPR / UK GDPR): Upon launching the app in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK, you are presented with the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog, allowing you to accept or refuse personalized advertising.
  • US State Privacy Laws (CCPA / CPRA / VCDPA): Residents of California, Virginia, and other US states have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data for targeted advertising.
  • Changing Your Choices: You can reset or update your consent preferences at any time by tapping Reset Privacy Consent under the Consent & Privacy section in the TypeRush Settings screen.

5. Strict Ad Placement Policy

To preserve your typing flow and prevent accidental taps:

  • Zero Ads During Tests: The active typing test screen is 100% ad-free.
  • Capped Frequency: Interstitial full-screen ads are capped to at most once every 3 completed tests and at least 3 minutes apart.

6. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

TypeRush is designed for general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data to third parties through our app, please contact us immediately so we can investigate and facilitate deletion.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all gameplay metrics and settings are stored locally on your device:

  • You can reset your metrics via the in-app Clear All History button.
  • Uninstalling the TypeRush application automatically purges all locally stored data.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates, technical changes, or legal obligations. When revised, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will be updated. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please reach out to us:
