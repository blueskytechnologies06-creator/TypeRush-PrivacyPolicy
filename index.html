Privacy Policy for TypeRush

Effective Date: November 17, 2026 Application Name: TypeRush Developer: BlueSky Technologies Contact: blueskytechnologies06@gmail.com
Privacy Guarantee: TypeRush is built with a privacy-first, device-isolated architecture. All typing tests, speed calculations, scores, and custom palettes stay strictly on your device. We do not require account registration, collect personal profiles, or synchronize your private typing records to any external cloud server.

1. Introduction & Core Principles

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which TypeRush, published by BlueSky Technologies, handles user data. We are committed to complete transparency, user security, and ethical advertising standards.

Unlike apps that require mandatory registrations, social logins, and continuous cloud telemetry, TypeRush functions entirely from your local device storage.

2. Information We DO NOT Collect

To provide a safe, frictionless experience, TypeRush never asks for or collects:

  • Personal Identity: Your real name, home address, phone number, or personal contacts.
  • Account Credentials: There is no user registration, password, or cloud profile system.
  • Keystroke Logging Outside Tests: The app does not capture, record, or transmit keystrokes entered outside the active test arena.
  • Financial Data: We do not ask for or store credit cards, bank accounts, or billing information.
  • Sensory Hardware: We do not access your microphone, camera, photo gallery, or device sensors.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

TypeRush stores functional performance data directly within your private device sandbox using high-performance, encrypted MMKV local storage:

  • Typing Performance Metrics: Net Words Per Minute (WPM), Raw WPM, accuracy percentage, error counts, and test durations.
  • Historical Progression: Completed typing tests and milestone achievements.
  • App Preferences: Selected theme (Dark, Light, System), active color palette (Emerald, Cyberpunk, Synthwave, Monokai Pro, Dracula, Nord), sound effects, and haptic feedback toggles.
  • Cosmetic Unlocks: Digital unlock status of bonus theme palettes unlocked by voluntarily watching rewarded video ads.

All locally stored metrics never leave your device and cannot be accessed by BlueSky Technologies. You can erase all local records at any time by navigating to Settings > Clear All History inside the app.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

TypeRush is supported by advertising delivered through Google AdMob. While TypeRush itself does not collect personal identifiers, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect certain device data to serve advertisements:

  • Mobile Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID) on Android devices, and Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iOS (subject to user consent).
  • Technical Device Information: Device model, operating system version, carrier, and screen dimensions.
  • Coarse Location Data: IP address used to approximate general regional geographic areas for relevant regional advertising.
  • Ad Interaction Metrics: Information regarding ad impressions, views, clicks, and diagnostic performance logs.

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services

5. User Consent & Privacy Controls (Google UMP)

We respect your right to control ad personalization:

  • European Regulations (GDPR / UK GDPR): Upon launching the app in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK, you are presented with the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog, allowing you to accept or refuse personalized advertising.
  • US State Privacy Laws (CCPA / CPRA / VCDPA): Residents of California, Virginia, and other US states have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data for targeted advertising.
  • Updating Your Preferences: You can reset or update your consent preferences at any time by tapping Reset Privacy Consent under the Consent & Privacy section in the TypeRush Settings screen.

6. Strict Ad Placement Policy

To preserve your typing flow and ensure an uninterrupted user experience:

  • Zero Ads During Active Tests: The typing test arena is 100% ad-free during typing practice.
  • Capped Frequency: Interstitial full-screen ads are capped to at most once every 3 completed tests and at least 3 minutes apart.
